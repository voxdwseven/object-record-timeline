The AI Timeline

Free, no subscription

Why I built it. AI governance arguments keep skipping the history: who decided what, when, and why it mattered. This puts the full run on one page, from the first print-licensing laws to the current AI rulebook fights.

What it does. A sourced, graded record from 1455 to 2026, each entry tagged by era and by whether it is a word (law, paper, policy) or a practice (a machine, a deployed system), on two spines: technology and governance. A prologue traces print-era precedent (the Stationers' Company, the Licensing Act, the Statute of Anne) before the main run opens in 1943.

The Object Record

Free Substack subscription

I built this to tell Jewish history by what survives, and to judge every claim by the evidence behind it. Five tools sit here, plus a references page. All run on the same rule: a real object or a dated document counts, hearsay does not, and scripture read as history does not.

The evidence standard
Acontemporary object or document
Baccount within a lifetime
Ctradition written later
Xcontested by specialists

The Object Record Timeline

What it does. All 207 objects on a time spine, grouped into eight eras, opening around 1208 BCE and running to 2026. Open any entry for what the object is, what happened, who acted, and what it changed.

Israel and Gaza in Focus

What it does. The same evidence standard, narrowed to the 1780s through the war in Gaza today: emancipation and nationalism, the Mandate and the Holocaust, 1948 and the Nakba, the wars, and the record cited on both sides.

The Reprisal Line

What it does. Fifty-eight sourced events on two lanes, Palestinian and Arab-side actions on one, Israeli-side actions on the other. Curved lines connect an action to the response it is sourced as having triggered.

The Charge Line

What it does. Two lanes, texts that asserted the Christ-killer charge on one side and the rulers, councils, and statutes that enforced it on the other, from a 3rd-century-BCE Egyptian slander to the Nuremberg Laws.

A Roadmap of Hate

What it does. Traces how the same charges and symbols recur across centuries and movements.

The point

The goal is to eliminate ignorance, my own first, by putting the evidence where you can see it and grade it yourself. No favorites. No unsourced numbers. Where specialists contest a record, it says so, and you decide.
