How to use this
Cards
Every card is a real document or event from the last 250 years. Click one to expand it; where a photograph exists, a More detail button opens the full record with the image. Filter or search to narrow the set.
The grade
A is a contemporary document, B an account within a lifetime, X contested by specialists.
Quiz
Short questions that show you a document and ask what it established.
Progress
What you have marked as learned, and how far through you are.
Filters
The dropdowns narrow the list. The i on each says what it covers.
Jump to era
Scrolls to the first card of that period.

Filters narrows search by era, actor, grade, or photo.

Era iThe three modern periods this app covers: emancipation and nationalism, the Mandate and the Holocaust, and Israel and the diaspora.
Evidence grade iHow strong the evidence is: A a contemporary document, B an account within a lifetime, X contested by specialists.
Actor iWho acted in the event, such as a state, a community, or an Arab or Palestinian actor.
Hostility type iThe kind of hostility an event documents, such as racial, conspiratorial, or nationalist. A description of what the source shows, with no judgment attached.
Presence iThe event's relation to the land: self-rule, presence, removal, return, foreign rule, or diaspora.
Photograph iWhether the entry shows a photograph.
Progress iWhether you have marked the event as learned.

A document appears; you say what it established. Answers are the outcome on record.
