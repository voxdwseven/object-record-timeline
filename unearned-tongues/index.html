How to use this
Cards
Every card is a dated document or event from the book, one chapter at a time. Click a card to expand it; a photograph, where one exists, opens larger if you click it.
The grade
A is a primary document, court record, statute, or contemporary photograph. B is an account established by a named historian within decades, cited to their published work. X is a claim the book itself flags as contested or disputed.
Filters

Every card names its source. Quotations are transcribed verbatim from the primary document, opinion, or manuscript named in the source line. Where a figure is drawn from the book's own charts, the chart's sources are given in full. This board covers the whole book, all seven chapters; it is not a complete history of any one of them.
